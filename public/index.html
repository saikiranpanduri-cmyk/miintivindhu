Our Story

Started in a family kitchen.

Mee Inti Vindhu — "a feast from your home" — began when friends living away from home kept asking for a plate of real, everyday Telugu food.

Today we cook for students, working professionals and families who miss the taste of home, keeping the same recipes, the same care and the same small batches.

“Eating this pappu and avakaya took me straight back to my grandmother's house.” — A happy regular
